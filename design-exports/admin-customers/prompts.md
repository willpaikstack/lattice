## OPTION 1 — DIRECTORY + TABS

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a very high fidelity product design comparison board with TWO large desktop interface screenshots, CUSTOMER DIRECTORY on the left and COMPANY DETAIL on the right, each occupying half of a wide landscape canvas. Make small text readable, prioritize actual content and intentional hierarchy. Screens represent Lattice's internal manufacturing customer administration.
Visual system: graphite #19232f sidebar, warm ivory #f8f9f7 canvas, white panels, cobalt #245be8 actions, slate typography, thin neutral borders, subtle green status pills; modern, crisp, restrained business software. Keep the official Lattice brand: uppercase LATTICE wordmark and simple existing outer diamond with four diamond cells, no newly invented logo. Same sidebar on both screens: Quote Submissions, Customers (selected), Overseas Vendors, Placed Orders, Material Inquiries, Resources.
Real data shape: directory rows are COMPANIES, not individual people. Columns: Company (initials icon, company name, website, contact), Industry (user count beneath), RFQs (total and active), Orders (count and supplier-shop count), Tier, Status. Search and filters All, Active, Standard, With orders. Existing separate Waiting list has Name, Email, Company, Need, Joined; keep access to it but give customer directory priority. Existing Create customer creates a company and first Customer Admin; put this in one clear primary CTA.
Company fields: Business name, Website, Industry, Primary contact, Primary contact email, Billing email, Customer tier, Account status, Internal profile notes. A Company owns Users and Requests. User records show name, work email, Customer Admin or Customer Member role and password/setup status; support Add user, change role, reset and resend invitation, set custom password, verified email change, Remove user, View customer workspace. Put sensitive actions inside a deliberate per-user menu or edit panel, never exposed password inputs in the default view. Request history contains RFQs and purchased orders with title, status, requester, due/updated dates, quote price and lead time, files, supplier and line item information. Show representative compact rows with click-through detail.
Use FICTIONAL example companies and contacts only: Atlas Precision (atlas.example), Northstar Machining, Meridian Robotics. Selected company Atlas Precision, contact Jordan Lee, jordan@atlas.example, billing ap@atlas.example, industry Precision machining, Standard tier, Active status. Example metrics 12 RFQs, 3 active, 4 orders, 2 users. Do not invent payment terms, credit limits, revenue trends, integrations or permissions beyond this schema.
Constraints: these are proposed layout mockups, not a marketing page; no giant analytics charts, gradients, decorative pictures, unauthorized customer logos, fake percent trends, device frames, or watermarks. Exact important labels must be legible.
OPTION 1 — DIRECTORY + TABS
Directory: a strong Customers heading with Create customer CTA, a discreet Companies / Waiting list switch, restrained single-line search/filter toolbar and clear six-column table. Remove repetitive status labels, excessive fallback text and full-width provisioning forms. Table is the main content.

Modern workspace with clear information priority.
