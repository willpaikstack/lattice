## Single-selector temper/condition follow-up

The separate Condition dropdown is removed. Browse and search share all 300 exact options and retain all 104 legacy selectable RFQ keys. Seventeen existing aluminum condition choices remain distinct: 2014-T6/T651, 2017-T4, 2024-T3/T351, 5052-H32, 5083-H111, 6060-T6, 6061-T6/T651, 6063-T5/T6, 6082-T6/T651 and 7075-T6/T651/T7351. Existing unspecified aluminum selections are not assigned one of those tempers automatically.

This preserves the selected label; it does not certify a supplier's heat treatment. Unnamed conditions stay unspecified. Brass H59/H62 and PC+ABS/T85 are not parsed as tempers.
