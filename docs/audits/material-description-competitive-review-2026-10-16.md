# Material description competitive review

**Date:** 2026-10-16  
**Scope:** Customer-facing material index and family-guide descriptions.  
**Match rule:** Broad family coverage is acceptable, as directed. Grade-level replacements are limited to featured Lattice grades with an identifiable match or coverage in the public comparator catalogs.  
**Copy approach:** The wording below is original Lattice copy informed by the materials, grade lists, and application themes published by the sources. It is not copied verbatim. Competitor listings are evidence of catalog coverage, not proof of Lattice stock, certification, or production capability.

## What changed

Replaced the short family summary on the materials index for all 12 families, and replaced 34 featured grade descriptions in their family guides. Grade names, catalog counts, forms, machinability ratings, selection notes, mechanical-property data, and availability language were not changed.

### Family summaries replaced

| Lattice family | New index summary |
| --- | --- |
| Aluminum | Aluminum grades span lightweight structural alloys, corrosion-resistant sheet, and high-strength aerospace stock; temper and product form determine the right fit. |
| Stainless steel | Stainless grades range from general-purpose 304/304L and chloride-resistant 316/316L to free-machining, duplex, martensitic, and precipitation-hardened options. |
| Mild steel | Low-carbon and structural steels support economical fabrication and machining; choose by strength, weldability, stock form, and governing specification. |
| Brass | Brass options range from free-machining bar stock to formable and marine grades for fittings, electrical parts, and hardware. |
| Copper | Copper grades serve electrical and thermal applications, with high-conductivity stock as well as alloys designed for forming or added strength. |
| Alloy steel | Alloy steels cover heat-treatable grades for shafts, gears, and loaded parts; strength and machinability depend on the specified heat-treatment condition. |
| Tool steel | Tool steels are selected by wear, toughness, hot-work duty, and heat-treatment condition for dies, molds, punches, and production tooling. |
| Titanium | Commercially pure and alloyed titanium grades offer different balances of corrosion resistance, strength, formability, and weight for industrial and aerospace parts. |
| Inconel / Incoloy | Nickel alloys are used in hot, corrosive, or high-load service; grade and condition determine temperature capability and fabrication requirements. |
| Precision alloys | Precision alloys support controlled thermal expansion or magnetic response in seals, instruments, electronics, and stable structures. |
| Magnesium / zinc | Magnesium and zinc offerings include lightweight wrought stock and die-casting alloys for housings, brackets, and detailed components. |
| Plastics / polymers | Engineering plastics range from low-friction wear materials to chemical-resistant and high-temperature resins for machined components. |

## Comparator sources

- [Xometry CNC machining materials](https://www.xometry.com/capabilities/cnc-machining-service/) and [Xometry materials by category](https://community.xometry.com/kb/articles/637-materials-by-category): broad machining catalog coverage across metals and plastics; specific steel and material references also include [steel CNC machining](https://www.xometry.com/capabilities/cnc-machining-service/cnc-steel/) and [304 stainless steel](https://www.xometry.com/resources/materials/304-stainless-steel/).
- [Fictiv Materials and Finishes](https://www.fictiv.com/materials-and-finishes) and [Fictiv CNC stainless steel](https://www.fictiv.com/materials/cnc-stainless-steel): material and grade coverage with application context for CNC materials.
- [Hubs / Protolabs Network CNC machining](https://www.hubs.com/cnc-machining/) and [metals for manufacturing](https://www.hubs.com/knowledge-base/metals-for-manufacturing/): broad material catalog and selection guidance. Hubs is treated here as Protolabs Network.
- [Protolabs CNC machining materials](https://www.protolabs.com/materials/cnc-machining/): a separate Protolabs material comparison reference for common CNC grades.

## Limits

Competitor pages vary in depth and are not a complete, uniform grade-by-grade description set. Broad-family matches were used for family summaries; exact designations were used when the public catalogs identified them. Descriptions remain application guidance, not engineering approval. Customers must specify the required standard, grade, temper/condition, form, and documentation in the RFQ; actual availability and certification are confirmed during review.
