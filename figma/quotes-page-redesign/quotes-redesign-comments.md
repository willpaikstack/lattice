# Quotes Page Redesign Comments

## Keep

- Keep the Lattice OS sidebar structure and visual language consistent with the current app.
- Keep the page operational and dense enough for repeated buyer use.
- Keep quote rows clickable and clearly connected to quote detail pages.
- Keep the core data visible: RFQ reference, status, title, part/material, process, quantity, price, lead time, last updated date, and next step.
- Keep search as the only list control for now.

## Change

- Redesign the Quotes page so it feels calmer, more premium, and easier to scan.
- Improve the hierarchy between summary metrics and the quote list.
- Make statuses and next actions feel more intentional and less like generic table tags.
- Make pending values, buyer action items, quoted/priced rows, and purchased rows easier to distinguish at a glance.
- Improve row spacing, alignment, hover states, and empty/search states.
- Remove any visual affordance for status filtering; those filters are not part of the desired page for now.

## Avoid

- Do not turn this into a marketing page or oversized hero layout.
- Do not redesign the sidebar unless a very small adjustment is necessary for consistency.
- Do not add status filter chips/tabs back into the page.
- Do not hide critical quote state behind decorative cards.
