# Missing material reference data: research breakdown

Research date: October 19, 2026. Local picker snapshot: 190 options representing 189 distinct catalog grades (150 metals and 39 polymer/composite entries). Existing reference-bearing RFQ choices are outside this inventory. No application material metadata, saved requests or sourcing eligibility has been changed.

## Read this first

- **Reference ready**: a source-backed UNS and condensed composition can be used as reference metadata for the exact named grade. This does not certify a supplier, stock, a product specification or an individual heat.
- **Partial reference**: useful identity or composition found, but one field, product specification or variant remains unresolved. Do not import the row as a complete verified profile.
- **Identity review**: the current label is ambiguous, conflates specifications, or needs a trade-name confirmation. Any candidate values are conditional.
- **Further sourcing needed**: this pass did not establish enough primary evidence to publish the requested values. Source leads are explicitly identified; they are not citations proving missing values.
- **UNS not applicable**: polymer/composite entry; display resin and reinforcement metadata instead. UNS applies to metals and alloys.

Composition is **weight percent** where numeric metal chemistry is supplied. **Nominal** values describe a representative alloy and are not acceptance limits. Ranges and min/max retain their meanings. **Balance** is the remaining base metal. Summaries omit some trace elements and must link to the full source. A shorthand such as AlMg1SiCu is an alloy description, not a molecular formula or substitute for composition limits.

## Recommended use in the dropdown

Use `UNS: S32100 · Fe balance; Cr 17–19%; Ni 9–12%` for a verified reference. Provide complete chemistry in a details view. Mark nominal values explicitly. For EN/GB/JIS grades, show the verified native designation even where an exact UNS is not verified. For plastics, omit UNS and show resin/reinforcement. Missing reference metadata and sourcing eligibility must remain separate: adding chemistry does not make a sourcing-only offering an approved direct-quote choice.

## Findings that affect implementation

1. Store source URL, composition basis, standard/product form and verification status alongside UNS. Do not leave nominal chemistry indistinguishable from specification limits.
2. Preserve exact variants: 800/800H/800HT; 310/310S; S31803/S32205; 430F/430FR; ordinary 316L versus implant-grade 316LVM; 4140 heat conditions.
3. Confirm broad labels before adding values: Beryllium Copper, 410Mod, 1018S, FE510, S355, Nimonic 300, ZN and unqualified trade-name shorthand.
4. Correct or clarify suspect labels: A413 versus A413.0; QSN6-6-3 / CuSn6Zn6; HDPE 1000; POM / Delrin.
5. Keep native grade designations when no exact UNS cross-reference is established. A missing UNS does not make a valid EN/GB/JIS material invalid.
6. Source conflict found: SSINA lists Nitronic 50 with S21900, whereas the alloy producer identifies Nitronic 50 as S20910. Use the producer reference. Other approximate equivalence and chemistry-table inconsistencies are noted in individual rows.
7. Polymer formulae and unspecified blend recipes were deliberately not fabricated. For these options, manufacturer resin grades, filler content and formulation datasheets are the useful reference fields.
